How the Cooperative Marketplace Works

A four-step digital pipeline connecting households with vetted artisans while ensuring dignity and social security for workers.

1

Select Your Need

Choose from 10 certified trades. Choose a standard scheduled appointment or instant 30-min emergency SOS dispatch.

2

Cooperative Matching

The platform matches you with the closest police-verified worker affiliated with a registered District Labour Society.

3

Quality Service

The worker arrives with certified tools and genuine parts, adhering strictly to state-notified minimum fair wage rates.

4

Fair Pay & Welfare

Direct digital settlement with zero aggregator exploitation. 5% goes directly to the Worker Health & Pension Welfare Pool.

The Cooperative Advantage

Why Choose Cooperative Labour Over Private Aggregators?

Private service aggregators charge workers 25% to 35% in commissions and use opaque surge pricing. Sahakari Shramik operates on cooperative principles: owned by workers, governed by standards, and built for citizen safety.

Fair Standardized Wages

Every rate is benchmarked to state minimum skilled labour standards. No hidden surge pricing during rain or holidays.

Institutional Trust & Police Verification

Every worker is authenticated by their local Primary Labour Cooperative Society (PACS/LCS) and local police records.

Accidental Insurance & Welfare Pool

Workers are insured under group life and accidental disability schemes, providing dignity and protection for their families.
